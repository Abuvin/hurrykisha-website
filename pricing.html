Frequently asked questions

Do all plans include all six products?
+
Yes. Every plan — including Growth — includes all six products: Quotation Engine, Report Intelligence, Insight Mining, Document AI, Workflow Automation, and Decision Assistant. Plans differ only in volume limits and support levels, not in which products you can access. We believe feature-gating is dishonest.
What happens if I go over my monthly limits?
+
We notify you when you reach 80% of any limit. You can purchase add-on volume at any time (extra document pages at $0.002/page, extra quotes at $0.50/quote). We never auto-charge overages without your approval — the system will alert you and pause that specific function until you add capacity or upgrade. Nothing goes down unexpectedly.
How long does implementation take?
+
Most Growth plan customers are live within one week using our self-serve setup. Operations customers get a 2-week guided onboarding with our implementation team — we configure your integrations, set up your extraction schemas, and build your first three workflows with you. Enterprise deployments are typically 3–4 weeks from contract to go-live.
Can I start with one product and expand?
+
Yes, and we recommend it. Most teams start with either Quotation Engine or Document AI — whichever has the highest volume and most immediate ROI — and expand to additional products over the first 90 days. All products are available from day one; you simply activate and configure each one when you're ready. There's no additional charge to activate a product.
Is there a free trial?
+
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on Growth and Operations plans — no questions asked. We also offer a full-platform live demo (what you see at dashboard.html) so you can explore every product before committing. For Enterprise, we run a paid proof-of-concept engagement scoped to your specific processes before full contract.
What integrations are included?
+
Growth includes 20+ standard connectors: Salesforce, HubSpot, Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, Xero, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and more. Operations adds the full 100+ connector library plus the ability to request custom integrations (typically built within 2 weeks). Enterprise includes custom connector development with dedicated engineering support.
How is data handled and stored?
+
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We operate on AWS infrastructure in your chosen region (Africa, EU, or US). Your data is never used to train models or shared with third parties. Enterprise customers can opt for a dedicated deployment where their data never co-mingles with other tenants. Full data processing agreements available on request.
Do you offer a discount for annual billing?
+
Yes — 20% off when you pay annually. Growth goes from $990/month to $792/month ($9,504/year). Operations goes from $2,490/month to $1,992/month ($23,904/year). Toggle the billing switch above to see annual prices. Enterprise pricing is always negotiated annually.
