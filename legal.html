Legal & Privacy

HurryKisha AI Systems is committed to being transparent about how we handle your data. These documents govern your use of our platform and services. We've written them in plain language wherever possible.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how HurryKisha AI Systems ("HurryKisha", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our platform, products, and services.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account registration information (name, work email, company, role)
  • Contact and inquiry information submitted through our forms
  • Business data you upload for processing through our products (documents, data sources, integrations)
  • Usage data and platform activity (features used, queries made, workflows run)
  • Payment information (processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers)

How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our platform and products
  • To process documents, generate quotes, run reports, and power AI features on your behalf
  • To send service communications (product updates, billing, security notices)
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations
We do not use your business data to train AI models. Documents, queries, and data you process through HurryKisha products are used solely to deliver results to you. They are never used to train or improve our AI systems without your explicit written consent.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (cloud infrastructure, payment processing, email delivery) — under strict data processing agreements
  • AI model providers for processing queries — under zero-data-retention agreements where applicable
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety

Data retention

We retain account data for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days after termination. Processed document data is deleted within 30 days of processing unless you explicitly request longer retention. You can request immediate deletion at any time by contacting privacy@hurrykisha.ai.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data; to object to processing; and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at privacy@hurrykisha.ai to exercise any of these rights.

International data transfers

We operate primarily from Kenya and process data on AWS infrastructure in your chosen region (Africa, EU, or US). Where data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place including standard contractual clauses.

Terms of Service

By accessing or using HurryKisha's platform and services, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you are using HurryKisha on behalf of an organisation, you represent that you have authority to bind that organisation to these terms.

Acceptable use

You may use HurryKisha only for lawful business purposes. You agree not to:

  • Use the platform for any illegal or unauthorised purpose
  • Upload documents you do not have the right to process
  • Attempt to reverse engineer or extract our AI models
  • Use automated tools to scrape or abuse the platform
  • Resell or sublicense access without our written consent

Your data and content

You retain all rights to data and documents you upload. By using our services, you grant us a limited licence to process your data solely to deliver the services you've requested. We do not claim ownership of your content.

Service availability

We target 99.9% uptime for Enterprise plans and 99.5% for Growth and Operations plans. Scheduled maintenance will be communicated at least 24 hours in advance. We are not liable for downtime caused by factors outside our reasonable control.

14-day money-back guarantee: If you are unsatisfied with Growth or Operations plans within 14 days of initial subscription, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.

Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, HurryKisha's total liability to you for any claim arising from use of the platform shall not exceed the amounts paid by you in the 12 months preceding the claim. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

Termination

Either party may terminate the subscription at any time with 30 days notice. Upon termination, you retain access through the end of your paid billing period. We will make your data available for export for 90 days after termination, after which it will be permanently deleted.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Nairobi, Kenya, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

Cookie Policy

HurryKisha uses cookies and similar technologies to operate our website and platform. This policy explains what we use, why, and how to control them.

Essential cookies

Required for the platform to function. These cannot be disabled. They include session authentication, CSRF protection, and user preference storage. No opt-out is available as they are strictly necessary for service delivery.

Analytics cookies

We use privacy-respecting analytics (Plausible Analytics — no personal data, no cross-site tracking) to understand how our website is used. These can be blocked with any standard ad blocker without affecting platform functionality.

Marketing cookies

We do not use third-party marketing or advertising cookies. We do not participate in retargeting networks. If you see HurryKisha ads elsewhere, they are targeted by platform demographics, not by cookies from our site.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in to the platform. All other cookies can be blocked without affecting your ability to use HurryKisha.

Data Processing Agreement

For customers subject to GDPR, POPIA, or similar data protection regulations, HurryKisha acts as a data processor when handling personal data on your behalf. We offer a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that specifies the legal basis for processing, security measures, and sub-processor commitments.

To request a DPA, contact legal@hurrykisha.ai. Enterprise customers have DPA execution included in their contract process. Standard DPAs are available for Operations plan customers on request.

Sub-processors

We use the following key sub-processors in delivering our services:

  • Amazon Web Services — Cloud infrastructure and storage (EU-West, AF-South, US-East regions)
  • Anthropic — AI model provider for Decision Assistant and document analysis features (zero-data-retention agreement)
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Postmark — Transactional email delivery

We maintain a complete and current sub-processor list. Enterprise customers are notified of any sub-processor changes with 30 days notice.

Security

We take the security of your data seriously. The following measures are in place across all plans:

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 for all data in motion
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256 for all stored data
  • Access controls: Role-based access, least-privilege principle
  • Audit logging: All access and actions logged with timestamps
  • Vulnerability management: Regular penetration testing, dependency scanning
  • Incident response: Defined procedures with notification within 72 hours of confirmed breach

Enterprise customers additionally receive dedicated infrastructure, SOC 2 Type II reports on request, and custom security assessments.

To report a security vulnerability, please contact security@hurrykisha.ai. We operate a responsible disclosure policy and respond to all reports within 48 hours.
